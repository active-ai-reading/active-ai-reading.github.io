roles

The tasks you can take on for each paper. Pick one (or invent your own) every week.

Each week, everyone takes a task for that week’s paper and writes a short piece for the site. You can do the default version, or try something new that might fail. Both are good. Several people can do the same task; comparing versions is half the fun.

Before every class, read the paper: at least the main part, and whatever your task needs.

To claim a task, add yourself to that week in the schedule. To write it up, copy the template named after the role from the templates/ folder. The role and task ids below (in grey) are what you put in your post’s header.

✍️ Writing role: writing

Make the paper itself better written.

Rewrite the abstract (or title) task: abstract
Rewrite the abstract, or the title if that is what needs improving. Show before and after and say what you changed and why.
Copyedit a section task: copyedit
Copyedit the biggest part you can. Could the overall story improve? The structure? If not, what can be cut or phrased better? Give a before and after, and the general rule you applied.
The points that matter task: key-points
What are the most important points for most readers, and how could the paper convey or emphasize them better?

🧠 Learning to learn role: learning

Bring something new into the room.

Teach us something new task: teach
Teach us something new, on writing, on the topic, or on something else entirely.

📊 Visualization role: visualization

Make the paper's ideas visible.

Redo a graph, better task: redo-graph
Redo one of the paper's graphs, but better. In class we compare versions (and if several people did it, theirs too).
Visualize the core idea task: key-visual
Create a visualization of the most important part of the paper.

📣 Communicate to the world role: communication

Get the ideas out of the PDF and into people's feeds.

Social media posts task: social
Write a post for X, Bluesky, Mastodon and LinkedIn. Each platform has a different audience and length.
Compile the blog task: compile
Compile the week's outputs into one readable post that links to everyone's work.
One more step task: extra-step
Try something new, help the others, make a test, or use another platform.

🔬 Research skills role: research

Read like a researcher, not like a student.

Contact the authors task: contact-authors
Contact the authors with a question or a pointer, if it makes sense.
Critique task: critique
Strengths and weaknesses, and how the paper could have been improved. Decide if it is worth publishing, and worth pursuing.
Literature review task: literature
Which papers is this one built on? Which fields and concepts does it relate to most?
The authors' path task: authors
Describe the prominent authors (usually first and last) and how this paper fits into their larger research. Stick to public, professional information.
Follow-ups worth doing task: followups
Exercise academic taste. Suggest follow-ups that are worth doing, and say why.
A related paper from last month task: recent-paper
Find a relevant paper from the last month and explain its gist.

💻 Coding role: coding

Make it run.

Reimplement an experiment or graph task: reimplement
Reimplement an experiment, a metric, or a graph from the paper.

😎 Chill role: chill

We are a group. Sometimes it is not your job, and that is fine.

Chill, dude task: chill
Read the paper, come to class, ask good questions.

The roles live in _data/roles.yml. Edit that file to add, rename or remove roles and tasks; this page, the badges, and the paper pages all update automatically.
