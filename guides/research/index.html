🔬 The research skills guide

Written by the people who held this role. See also the latest handoffs.

Yours to write. Some questions to start from:

  • How do you critique fairly?

Tasks in this role

Sign up in the sign-up sheet.

  • Contact the authors (cancelled). Contact the authors with a question or a pointer, if it makes sense. Write
  • Critique. Strengths and weaknesses, and how the paper could have been improved. Decide if it is worth publishing, and worth pursuing. Write
  • Literature review. Which papers is this one built on? Which fields and concepts does it relate to most? Write
  • The authors' path. Describe the prominent authors (usually first and last) and how this paper fits into their larger research. Stick to public, professional information. Write
  • Follow-ups worth doing. Exercise academic taste. Suggest follow-ups that are worth doing, and say why. Write
