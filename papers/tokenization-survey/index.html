Tokenization: A Survey for Modern NLP

Marco Cognetta, Christopher Akiki, Pawan Sasanka Ammanamanchi, Catherine Arnett, Thomas Bauwens, Pavel Chizhov, Pieter Delobelle, Konstantin Dobler, Desmond Elliott, Omer Goldman, Tatsuya Hiraoka, Valentin Hofmann, Eugene Jang, Julie Kallini, İlker Kesen, Sachin Kumar, Sander Land, Jindřich Libovický, Tomasz Limisiewicz, Alisa Liu, Clara Meister, Benjamin Minixhofer, Sangwhan Moon, Tiago Pimentel, David Pohl, Suchir Salhan, Tomohiro Sawada, Pavel Stepachev, Gianluca Vico, Philip Whittington, Xiulin Yang, Yuval Pinter, 2026

Paper
TL;DR Tokenization is the fundamental step where text is split into the building blocks an LLM digests. It has implications for efficiency, multilingual balance, and security; this survey is both a high-level map of the field and a detailed reference for those who want to go deeper.
