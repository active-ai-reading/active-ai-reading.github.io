Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning: A Recurrent Depth Approach

Jonas Geiping, Sean McLeish, Neel Jain, John Kirchenbauer, Siddharth Singh, Brian R. Bartoldson, Bhavya Kailkhura, Abhinav Bhatele, Tom Goldstein, 2025

arXiv PDF alphaXiv discussion
TL;DR This paper suggests an efficient approach towards test-time compute scaling. While Chain-of-Thought has proven effective, it is wasteful in memory and compute by design, the authors suggest a latent reasoning approach instead, demonstrating its gains at scale.
