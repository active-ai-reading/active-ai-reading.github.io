This guide is yours to write. Some questions to start from (answer them, replace them, ignore them):

- What makes people stop scrolling, on each platform?
- How do you compress a paper into one claim without making it wrong?
- What did you post, and what actually happened (numbers, replies)?
- What did you try that didn't work?

## Starting points

Collected by the instructor to help the first people in this role. Keep what helps, add what you find.

- **Example paper threads by the instructor:** [tokenization](https://x.com/LChoshen/status/1569344058773983233), [linear mode connectivity](https://x.com/LChoshen/status/1491375423120613378), [choosing the best pretrained model](https://x.com/LChoshen/status/1587755377764057088). Look at how each opens and where the figure goes.
- **[Xthread2social](https://github.com/borgr/Xthread2social)**: republishes an X thread to Bluesky and Mastodon, splitting posts to each platform's length. Useful once you have written the X version, though each platform still deserves its own wording.
- **[The ever-growing writing guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14Wax8M5w8F_8miDlYJ9-I6wqpelxlXjCEUbkNzNMqqE/edit?usp=sharing)**: the "Abstract" section is about writing a promotional summary that leads with the takeaway, which is most of what a good first post does.
- **Example on this site:** [the paper in four feeds]({{ '/posts/2026/the-paper-in-four-feeds/' | relative_url }}).
