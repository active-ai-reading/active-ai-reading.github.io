This guide is yours to write. Some questions to start from (answer them, replace them, ignore them):

- How do you decide which figure to redo, and what is wrong with the original?
- Which tools did you use, and what would you use next time?
- How do you get the underlying numbers when the paper doesn't give them?
- What did you try that didn't work?

## Starting points

Collected by the instructor to help the first people in this role. Keep what helps, add what you find.

- **[The ever-growing writing guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14Wax8M5w8F_8miDlYJ9-I6wqpelxlXjCEUbkNzNMqqE/edit?usp=sharing)** (Google Doc), under *Common academic writing needs*: "Graphics" (make the main message visible at a glance), "Caption" (open with the figure's point), and under *Sections*, "Floating aids" (figures and tables should stand on their own).
- **Examples on this site:** [a redrawn Figure 2]({{ '/posts/2026/a-better-figure-2/' | relative_url }}) (an interactive chart you can copy) and [the core idea in one picture]({{ '/posts/2026/the-core-idea-in-one-picture/' | relative_url }}) (a diagram made with HTML).
