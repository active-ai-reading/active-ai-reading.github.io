This guide is empty on purpose: it is yours to write. Some questions to start from (answer them, replace them, ignore them):

- How do you critique fairly: what to look for first, what to ignore?
- How do you find a paper's ancestors and recent relatives quickly?
- What makes a follow-up idea worth doing?
- How did contacting authors go, and what would you say differently?
- What did you try that didn't work?
