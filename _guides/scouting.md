This guide is yours to write. Some questions to start from (answer them, replace them, ignore them):

- Where do you look for trending papers, and where for recent ones nobody is sharing yet?
- How do you decide a paper is of interest to this group?
- How do you write a one-sentence summary that is accurate and makes people want to read?
- What did you try that didn't work?

## Starting points

Collected by the instructor to help the first people in this role. Keep what helps, add what you find.

- **[paper_updated](https://borgr.github.io/paper_updated/)**: a curated list of ways to keep up with new papers.
  - *Trending papers:* ["What's trending"](https://borgr.github.io/paper_updated/#whats-trending) (alphaXiv, Bluesky, Papers with Code and more).
  - *Recent papers nobody is talking about:* ["Build a personal feed"](https://borgr.github.io/paper_updated/#build-a-personal-feed) (recommendation tools such as Scholar Inbox) and ["Cover a whole field"](https://borgr.github.io/paper_updated/#cover-a-whole-field) (arXiv category listings), where papers show up before anyone shares them.
