## How to scout trending papers

start from this week's paper rather than from a blank search. The papers most likely to interest the group are the ones that connect to something we're already reading.

1. **Pull out the key concepts.** Note the 2-3 main ideas, methods, or problems from this week's paper.
2. **Ask Claude for related papers.** Give Claude the paper and the concepts, and ask it to find recent papers on the same topics.
3. **Check the views for each candidate.** Look up how many views each paper has on arXiv.
4. **Pick the most viewed.** The paper with the highest views becomes the scouting pick.

Look for momentum, not just popularity. Check whether a paper is rapidly gaining attention rather than only looking at its total views. A recent paper whose views are growing quickly may be an emerging topic worth bringing to the group before it becomes widely known.
