This guide is empty on purpose: it is yours to write. Some questions to start from (answer them, replace them, ignore them):

- Where do you look for trending papers, and where for recent ones nobody is sharing yet?
- How do you decide a paper is of interest to this group?
- How do you write a one-sentence summary that is accurate and makes people want to read?
- What did you try that didn't work?
