# Paper scouting: Recent papers nobody is talking about

To avoid popularity metrics, I focused on [arxiv/Computation and Language](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent).
I found it hard to filter for compatibility with our forum, scrolling through ~600 papers no older than 2 weeks seemed inefficient.

** The references used in the Introductions **
What worked for me was noticing a certain recurring topic that drew my attention. 
Focusing on 3-4 Arxiv papers of a topic of my interest, I explored the papers mentioned in the introductions and related work sections.
I felt that the fact that a paper is referenced indicates interest in the field and points to more fundamental works.
