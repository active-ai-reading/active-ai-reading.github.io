# Handoffs

Notes from this week's people in a role to next week's. Not polished, not graded on success, not shown on the site.

**Where:** `handoffs/week-NN/<github-username>-<role>.md`
for example `handoffs/week-03/ohad-agadi-visualization.md`

**What** (any form; a few lines is enough):

- What I tried, especially anything new. Did it work?
- What was harder than expected, or failed.
- One thing I'd tell the next person: a tip, a tool, a trap.

**Who reads it:** the next person in your role. Before starting, they read the role's guide and its recent handoffs, and move what's worth keeping into the guide (`_guides/<role>.md`). Handoffs stay here as the raw record.
