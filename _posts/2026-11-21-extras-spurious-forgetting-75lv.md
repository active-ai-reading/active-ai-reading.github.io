## What's AIDA?
Slide posts are basically the modern day slideshow.
They can help present complex scientific topics, using an easy-to-digest and engaging format.

The AIDA method is used by many to maximize engagement and impact. In short, the structure is:

- **Attention.** Why should the viewer care? A strong hook to attract readers.
- **Interest.** The majority of the post. Should normally be the context. Try to maintain the interest of the reader.
- **Desire.** The main value of the post. 
- **Action.** An action for the viewer. Read more in our blog, engage in the comment sections, etc.

## Examples for posts

Here are some ideas for posts:

- **Everything you need to know about [insert paper idea].** Slides Explaining the paper. May contain a visualization made by someone.
- **Points we had to discuss about [insert paper name].** 3-5 slides about the main points discussed in class. Each slide contains a different point.
- **This is how you can improve your graphs (based on the graphs from [insert paper name]).** Slides showcasing someone's graph, what improved and how the reader can use this knowledge.
- **How [insert writing rule] can help your writing.** Slides about an actionable writing rule, ending with showcasing someone's writing.
