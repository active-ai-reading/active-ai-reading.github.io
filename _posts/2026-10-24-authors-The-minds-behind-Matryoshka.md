### Nathan Godey

Nathan Godey is a researcher in natural language processing and machine learning whose work focuses on language modeling, model representations, and training efficiency. His research explores how language models learn and how their architectures and training methods can be improved.

*Matryoshka Language Model Suites* fits closely into this research profile. The paper investigates how models of different sizes can be trained within a shared architecture, reducing training costs while maintaining performance. This extends his broader interest in developing more efficient and effective language models.

### Yoav Artzi

Yoav Artzi is a computer scientist and researcher in natural language processing and machine learning. His research has covered language understanding and generation, interactive language systems, and methods for training models to perform complex language tasks.

*Matryoshka Language Model Suites* fits into his broader research through its focus on improving language model training and deployment. Although the paper is more concerned with model architecture and computational efficiency than with interactive systems or language understanding, it aligns with his wider interest in advancing language modeling methods and making them more effective in practice.
